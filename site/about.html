About this map

The roadmap began as an ASCII diagram. This site draws the same routes as a transit map, with each tool linked to its own documentation.

How to read it

  • Everyone starts on Fundamentals.
  • At its end you can change to any other line. Lines are directions to specialise in, not a fixed order: most people ride more than one.
  • Stations are in a sensible order within each line, but you don't have to finish one before visiting the next.
